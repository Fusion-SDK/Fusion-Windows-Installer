# Code of Conduct

We want Fusion to be a welcoming, constructive community.

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Do not publish private information.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Follow project maintainers' directions in official spaces.

Reports can be sent privately to the repository maintainers. They will review reports fairly and take appropriate action.
